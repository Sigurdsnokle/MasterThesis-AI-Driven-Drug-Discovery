Prospective Validation of Machine Learning-Based Scoring Functions for Hit Expansion

Sigurd Skui Nøklebye
Master's Programme in Molecular Biology

About the thesis

This master's thesis evaluates the application of machine learning-based scoring functions for molecular docking and hit expansion in structure-based drug discovery.

Multiple machine learning scoring functions were evaluated for their ability to identify novel binders to the bacterial enzyme PaFabF C164A. Computational predictions were experimentally validated using grating-coupled interferometry (GCI) and X-ray crystallography.
